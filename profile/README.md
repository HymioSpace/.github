HymioSpace is building an open ecosystem for personal AI agents. Hymio brings your context, tools, and everyday interactions together so your agent can understand what matters to you and help across devices.

### What we're building

- **Hymio** — the app and experiences you interact with.
- **Oath** — the durable agent runtime that powers long-running work.
- **Nostalgia** — the personal context layer that organizes and retrieves what your agent needs to know.

### Why these names

Our names are inspired by [MONO](https://www.monoofjapan.com/), the post-rock band whose music has stayed with us.

- **Hymio** is our own music-inspired name: a personal agent should be in tune with you.
- **Oath** takes its name from [MONO's album and title track](https://monoofjapan.bandcamp.com/album/oath). It also reflects a commitment to carry work through, even across interruptions.
- **Nostalgia** takes its name from [MONO's song](https://monoofjapan.bandcamp.com/track/nostalgia). It evokes personal memory and the context that connects moments over time.

We're building these pieces in the open, with room for people to bring their own tools, extensions, and storage.
